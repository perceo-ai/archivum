# Modular Wood Desk Organizer + Glass Whiteboard — Mounting/Integration Plan

> Build-queue task: “Glass whiteboard integration plan (mounting, alignment, mounting hardware list)” (assigned_to: wrench)

## 1) Goal
Mount the glass/whiteboard panel so it:
- sits evenly on the organizer frame (no rocking)
- stays aligned over time (repeatable install/swap)
- maintains safe clearances for any power/power-strip components near the mounting plane
- preserves cable routing freedom (no crushed cord; no interference with moving/closing covers)

---

## 2) Choose a mounting approach
Pick one approach based on how your kit expects the panel to be held and what your frame geometry supports.

### Option A — Stand-off / standoff brackets (recommended when available)
**What it is:** small standoff brackets or spacers that hold the panel off the wood and control spacing.

**Why it’s good:**
- distributes load more evenly
- reduces direct wood-to-glass contact risk
- makes alignment repeatable by controlling height and gasket compression

### Option B — Bracket system (edge brackets or corner brackets)
**What it is:** corner/edge brackets that secure the panel at discrete points.

**Why it’s good:**
- simple when bracket positions are pre-defined
- good when your glass seating surface is narrow

### Option C — Rail / track style
**What it is:** panel edges captured by a rail/track (often with gaskets).

**Why it’s good:**
- very stable seating
- can simplify future swaps if the rail geometry is consistent

## 5) Mounting hardware list (template)
Use the kit’s manual for the exact sizes/types; below is the *checklist* of what you should confirm/collect.

### 5.1 Panel-to-frame interface
- Gaskets/pads (as specified by the kit)
- Spacers/standoffs (quantity = mounting points; size = per kit)
- Washers (matching screw/thread size)

### 5.2 Fasteners (per kit)
- Screws/bolts (size + length: ____)
- Threaded inserts for wood (if the kit uses them)
- Optional: threadlocker (only if the kit specifies)

### 5.3 Alignment aids
- Shims (thin, compressible or rigid—pick what your kit tolerates)
- Masking tape + marker for hole templates
- Template/measure alignment jig (if provided)

### 5.4 Clearance helpers (power/cable)
- Cable standoff(s) / cable guard clips (to keep cord away from the mounting plane)
- Strain relief / grommet(s) as provided

---

## 6) Drill/fasten procedure (safe sequence)
1. **Unplug** / confirm power module is out of the way.
2. Place the panel dry-fit.
3. Mark mounting points using the kit template (or measure from the captured geometry above).
4. Confirm drill path is clear of internal cable channels.
5. Drill only the required pilot holes and only to the kit-recommended depth.
6. Insert threaded inserts (if used by your approach).
7. Install mounting hardware with controlled torque (stop when gasket compression looks even).
8. Re-run alignment checks (Section 4) before final lock.

---

## 7) Final verification (before you power-on anything)
- Panel seats evenly; no rocking.
- All covers close without pinching the cord.
- Cords move freely through the intended travel/routing with the panel mounted.
- Power/power-strip components have the recorded clearances from Section 3.4.
